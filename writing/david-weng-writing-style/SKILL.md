---
name: david-weng-writing-style
description: 'Writes or reviews Chinese and English prose in David Weng''s direct, factual voice. Use when the user says 按我的风格. Not for technical docs or formal reports.'
---

**Voice:** direct, no filler — say it, stop. **解释即止** (a sentence or two per definition, no tangents). First person; 你 OK, never 我们/大家. Cold humor as garnish only. Emotion through facts. Criticism needs a factual cause.

**Structure:** fact-first openings; `##`/`###` only; short paragraphs; term format `**术语（全称，译名）** + 解释`. **DP entries:** action + result only (`## 日期 · 简短描述`), prerequisites as background, end at the result — no judgment, no summary. Links inline, never explained. DP/timeline/guide articles go entirely in one code block (frontmatter + body) for copy-paste.

**Sentences:** short first; no em dash, no semicolons. Half-width space between Chinese and English/numbers. Technical terms get Chinese on first mention. Ban 完事/说白了/顺便说一句, cheesy metaphors, slang, pseudo-intimacy.

**Guides:** no color, no padding (ban 顾名思义/简单来说), objective labels, `日期 · 动作` + actor/target/amount/result.

**Moves:** direct stance everywhere; humor/tips OK in narratives, minimal in guides, banned in DP. No rhetorical questions or closings in guides/DP.

**Blacklist:** cheesy closings (希望这篇对你有帮助), 大家/小伙伴们, summaries (以上就是……), process commentary (过程顺利). **Typography:** full-width `，。？！「」…`, half-width English; official casing (`GitHub`, `iOS`, `WeChat`); 登录/账号; lowercase `internet`.
